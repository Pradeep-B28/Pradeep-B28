### 🚀 Founder of Softech & Technical L&D Architect

> **Welcome! I am Pradeep B, Founder of [Softech](https://reachsoftech.onrender.com/home) and Learning & Development Lead.**
> 
> At **Softech** (**[https://reachsoftech.onrender.com/home](https://reachsoftech.onrender.com/home)**), users and students can **find job opportunities, placement drives, and enroll in industry-aligned technical courses** covering Java & Data Structures, Full Stack Web Development, MERN Stack, AWS Cloud Computing, and System Design.

- 🏢 **Founder of Softech**: Built and architected the Softech career application portal connecting students with corporate hiring drives and technical bootcamps.
- 🎓 **Mentored 7,500+ Engineering Candidates**: Led campus-to-corporate placement training across India's premier engineering institutions (VIT University, SIT Madurai, KSR Institutions), elevating placement rates from 70% to 90%.
- 👥 **Trainer Leadership**: Managed and led a team of 25 technical trainers delivering high-impact bootcamps.
- 💡 **Open-Source & WebGL Tools**: Creator of multi-theme 3D developer portfolios, database risk analyzers, AI release note generators, and devcontainer environments.
